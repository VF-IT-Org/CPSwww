CPS Distribution Service

About Us

CPS began operations in 1984 in Woodstown, New Jersey, providing USDA inspection services for meat imports, primarily from South America.

After the passing of original owner John Ober in 2021, CPS has continued under the leadership of his niece, Suzanne Schaefer, who has been part of the company since 1990.

Today, CPS focuses on warehousing, import and export support, refrigerated and non-refrigerated food storage, short- and long-term storage, and cross-docking services.

CPS is strategically located 11 miles from Salem and 29 miles from Baltimore, helping customers move cargo efficiently.

CPS is bonded, adding flexibility for customers who require bonded storage as part of their logistics operations.

Services Offered

  • 20,000 sq. ft. of dry warehousing
  • 15,000 sq. ft. of frozen storage
  • 5,000 sq. ft. of chilled storage
  • 3,000 sq. ft. loading dock with 5 container doors
  • Product inspection, labeling, and repacking
  • USDA meat inspection for export
  • Bonded warehousing services
  • Container haulage to and from the pier
  • Rail service with a private rail track
  • Online access to inventory through our 3PL WMS at www.extensiv.com

CPS offers highly competitive rates backed by a small, dedicated staff with many years of experience.

CPS can provide equipment for a wide range of cargo needs, including 20/40/45/48/53 containers, dry containers, reefers, flat racks, flatbeds, break bulk, and project cargo.

CPS supports businesses shipping anything from a single container to hundreds of containers. Trust CPS to keep your cargo moving.

We offer a wide range of services that can be adapted to your specific needs, whether you require dedicated space, special handling, or both.
