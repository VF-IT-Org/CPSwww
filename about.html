About CPS

Experienced logistics support since 1984.

CPS Distribution Service combines decades of warehousing experience with bonded storage, cold chain capability, inspection support, and direct transportation connections.

Our story

A long-standing operation built around service and flexibility.

1984

CPS began operations in Woodstown, New Jersey, supporting USDA inspection services for imported meat products.

1990 onward

Suzanne Schaefer joined the business and helped shape the customer-focused approach that still defines operations today.

Today

CPS provides dry, chilled, and frozen storage, cross-docking, import and export support, and responsive handling for a wide range of cargo.

Why CPS

Capability that goes beyond simple storage.

Bonded warehousing

CPS is bonded, giving customers added flexibility when handling imported cargo and special storage requirements.

Cold and dry storage

20,000 sq. ft. of dry storage, 15,000 sq. ft. of frozen storage, and 5,000 sq. ft. of chilled storage in one site.

Inspection and handling

Product inspection, labeling, repacking, and USDA meat inspection support for export and import workflows.

Transportation support

Container haulage to and from the pier plus support for dry containers, reefers, flat racks, flatbeds, break bulk, and project cargo.

Rail access

Private on-site rail connectivity helps support broader freight movement and cargo flexibility.

Inventory visibility

Customers can view inventory online through the Extensiv 3PL warehouse management platform.
